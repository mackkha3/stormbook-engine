The revenue leak

The problem isn’t your leads.
It’s what happens after they hit your inbox.

You spend thousands every month on Google Ads, LSAs, Facebook, and referrals. That machine works — you’re getting leads. But somewhere between the lead hitting your form and someone from your team calling them back, you’re leaking money to the roofer down the road who happened to be at his desk when the notification came in.

After 5 PM, after a storm, on a Sunday — those are the leads your competitor is winning while your team is at dinner.

Straight answers

Answers to what most contractors ask us

Still have a question? Bring it to the audit. You’ll get a direct answer, not a scripted objection handler.

Is this going to say something weird to my customers?

No live message is improvised without guardrails. Every customer-facing message comes from a template library you review and approve before launch. You control the brand voice, service-area language, qualification questions, and appointment rules.

How is this different from GoHighLevel, ServiceTitan, or Podium?

Those are broad software platforms that your team has to configure and manage. Stormbook is a done-for-you service built around one job in one vertical: turning inbound roofing leads into booked inspections, 24/7, under your brand. Your team receives the appointment instead of another tool to operate.

What integrations do you support?

Website forms, Google Local Services, Facebook lead ads, call-tracking numbers, and most major roofing CRMs. Setup includes the technical connection work, message approvals, calendar rules, and testing before launch.

Do you offer AI voice calling?

Yes. Voice AI is available with Premium. It can call leads who do not respond to text or email, follow your approved conversation framework, qualify the homeowner, and hand the conversation to your team when needed.

What if my current close rate is fine?

Then Stormbook may not be for you right now, and we’ll tell you that during the audit. If your first-response time is consistently under five minutes, 24/7, and your after-hours leads already book reliably, we’re solving a problem you may not have.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

We review response time, contact rate, appointments booked, lead quality, and operational fit. If the numbers work, we agree on the next phase. If they don’t, we shake hands and part ways. No auto-renewal and no games.
